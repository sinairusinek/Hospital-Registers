## map-govhosp-environs-1941.jpg

**Government Hospital & Environs — Haifa**, scale 1:1250, dated **19 September 1941**.
Town Planning Adviser's Plan No. **TP/389/41**, ref Z/725/36, drawn by E.L.S.

Shows the Government Hospital in its plot with the Carmelite Convent, the proposed Corniche
Road, the Haifa Harbour Extension, proposed railway lines and an old cemetery; the adjoining
landholders **Edgar Clark** and **Fu'ad Sa'ad** are named on the sheet, and two parcels are
marked State Domain (D/HAI/95, D/HAI/313). Based on D.L.S. plan 48/40 of 21.6.1940 and Haifa
L.R.S. plan 320/39/H of 11.12.1939; zoning from the approved Haifa–El-Attika Detailed Town
Planning Scheme No. 510, published in *Palestine Gazette* 764 of 3.3.1938.

**Credit as:** Israel State Archives, **מפה-4985/1**, Plan TP/389/41, 19.9.1941.
A Mandate Government of Palestine plan. Publication cleared 2026-08-28.

Full-resolution original (6042 × 6432 px PNG) is kept out of the repo at
`paper/sources/isa/figures/map_govhosp_environs_1941.png`; the file here is downscaled to
2400 px for the web.
